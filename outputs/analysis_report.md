# Supermarket Sales Analysis

## Dataset
- Transactions: 500
- Total sales: ₹244,411.08
- Average transaction: ₹488.82
- Average rating: 3.99/5

## Key Findings
- Highest-sales product: Cheese — ₹27,906.30
- Best branch: Branch C (Mumbai) — ₹72,469.45
- Highest-sales category: Beverages — ₹56,108.24
- Most-used payment method: UPI — 127 transactions
- Member average transaction: ₹483.14
- Normal average transaction: ₹497.07

## Business Recommendations
1. Maintain sufficient stock for high-sales products and categories.
2. Study the practices behind Branch C's strong sales performance.
3. Continue supporting UPI because it is the most-used payment method.
4. Use customer-rating feedback to improve service quality.
5. Compare Member and Normal customer spending when designing membership offers.
